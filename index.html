About Charles

From architecture to events.

I started in architecture and 3D visualisation. When I joined Sela as a design architect in 2019, I began working on entertainment projects. That was how I found my way into events, and the work I wanted to keep doing.

Today my work covers concept design, event identity and visual content, including projection mapping. I work with our designers, production teams and partners to take an idea from the first sketch through to the live event.

Arena · 2025 · Event Creative Director

Riyadh Comedy Festival

Biggest Comedy Festival in the WORLD!!!

Role
Event Creative Director
Year
2025
Where
Riyadh
Scale
50+ comedians · 4 theatres

The brief

Riyadh Season wanted the biggest comedy festival ever staged: more than fifty comedians across four theatres. I was asked to shape the creative vision and the audience experience across all of it.

The idea

Build the festival as one world rather than a run of shows: a mascot with a personality, a merchandising line people would actually wear, and environmental theming that turned the streets into part of the joke.

The hard part

Four theatre concepts, each through every revision round, and an approval process with more than fifty comedians and their teams, while the show visuals, projection mapping and immersive content were in production.

The result

Four themed theatres, one identity across every touchpoint, and a festival billed as the biggest comedy festival in the world.

Six Kings Slam 2

Six kings. One crown. A live show on a global stage.

Role
Event Creative Director
Year
2025
Where
Riyadh · BLVD World

The brief

A bigger event and a bigger challenge: the opening ceremonies of the Six Kings Slam, broadcast live on Netflix.

The idea

Six players staged as kings, and a show that left the arena: facade projection mapping across the venue, promos on the BLVD World Sphere, and a fan zone and VIP journey that felt like one premium world.

The hard part

Two audiences at once. With the international broadcast team, the show was directed for the people in the seats and for the Netflix cameras, with camera direction and storytelling planned together.

The result

“Production Value A++” (The Tennis Letter), “a five-minute cinematic experience with the best of the best” (The Tennis Channel), and “a world-class opening for a world-class event” in the words of H.E. Turki Al Alshikh.

Court · 2024 · Event Creative Director

Six Kings Slam 1

The first time hologram technology was used in a tennis event.

Role
Event Creative Director
Year
2024
Shows
6 opening shows + 1 branding show
Timeline
Under 25 days

The brief

The first edition. Ideation and creative direction for the Opening Match Show and the Pre-Awarding Ceremony: narrative flow, visual style and show structure.

The idea

Six players as warriors from six worlds, projection-mapped courts as their arenas, and the first hologram ever used in a tennis event.

The hard part

Six different opening shows and one branding show, all developed in under twenty-five days, alongside every print and on-site graphic, sponsor placement and the fan zone.

The result

A first for the sport, delivered on time, with branding, motion, print and on-site graphics reading as one system across the venue.

Usyk × Fury 2 Reignited

the concept was time. so we built a clock.

Role
Event Creative Director
Date
21 December 2024
Where
Kingdom Arena, Riyadh
Live audience
25,000+ spectators

The brief

An opening show for Usyk × Fury 2 that more than 25,000 people would experience live inside Kingdom Arena on 21 December 2024.

The idea

The concept was time. A clock the size of a stage between blue curtains, and a show built around the countdown to the first bell.

The hard part

Effects like this are normally only possible in post-production for the TV audience. Here the illusion had to happen once, live, in the room, in step with the promoters and broadcasters.

The result

A fully immersive live opening for more than 25,000 spectators, plus the full event branding and spatial design: fences, venue wraps, stage visuals, sponsor placements, accreditations, signage and access flows.

The Sphere

We turned the sphere transparent!

Role
Creative Director · content
Years
Four years of content
Where
BLVD World, Riyadh
Record
Biggest LED sphere in the world, before Vegas

In motion

See it happen.

The brief

Content for the Sphere in BLVD World, the previous record holder for the biggest LED sphere in the world before the Vegas Sphere.

The idea

Treat the Sphere as a canvas that can change its nature. At one point we turned it transparent.

The hard part

In the early weeks of operation the screen had imperfections. A diamond pattern was designed into the content to hide them.

The result

Four years of content creation and curation, the last three as the person responsible for everything shown on the Sphere.
