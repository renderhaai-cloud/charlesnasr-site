The architect

Buildings shape how people live. Experiences shape how they feel.

I trained as an architect with a strong interest in technology, and started as a visualiser and 3D artist across architecture and interior design. Seven years ago I joined Sela as a design architect, and that's where things shifted: I was introduced to the world of entertainment and events, and I realised this is where I belong. The scale, the freedom and the impact felt different.

Today I lead three territories at Sela: ideation and concept design; graphics and event identity; visual and media content, including projection mapping. For the last two years I've been Creative Director on some of the biggest entertainment projects in the region and the world.
